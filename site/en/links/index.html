More sources

A small collection of sites we read ourselves and find useful. Each one was checked individually: reachable, operator identifiable, freshness visible.

The collection has no influence on the Dreier Gauge. What does feed into it is marked “in the Dreier Gauge”. The links are neither paid nor brokered.

Situation reports from the German-speaking region

BSI: Die Lage der IT-Sicherheit in Deutschland

The annual report of Germany's federal cybersecurity agency. According to ZDFheute, the latest edition was presented on 19 November 2026 and rated the situation “tense to critical”.

Operator: Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik · Upkeep: official, annual · checked on 26 Nov 2026

CERT-Bund: Warn- und Informationsdienst (WID)in the Dreier Gauge

Continuous news on vulnerabilities, patches and workarounds for common IT products, filterable by product and severity. The advisories feed into the Dreier Gauge.

Operator: Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (CERT-Bund) · Upkeep: official, ongoing · checked on 26 Nov 2026

BKA: Bundeslagebild Cybercrime

Annual analysis of cybercrimes recorded by the police. For 2025 the report counts about 334,000 cases. Only reported offences are included; the unreported share is larger.

Operator: Bundeskriminalamt · Upkeep: official, annual · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Swisscom Cybersecurity Threat Radar

Annual report on the threat situation in Switzerland, for trends, without daily values.

Operator: Swisscom · Upkeep: annual · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Municipalities and public administration

Kommunaler Notbetrieb

Overview of publicly documented IT security incidents in German municipalities, with a timeline and case cards. The site does not name a source per incident; content under CC BY-SA 4.0, RSS feed only.

Operator: Jens Lange (Privatprojekt, laut Tagesspiegel Background hauptberuflich Informationssicherheitsbeauftragter einer Stadtverwaltung) · Upkeep: by hand, from public reports · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Kommunalwiki: Cyberangriffe

One long wiki article with more than 60 individual municipal cases from 2016 to 2026, each with footnotes, mostly press reports. No change date and no licence in the retrieved text.

Operator: Kommunalwiki (Links führen zur Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung) · Upkeep: by hand, open wiki · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Attacks and network, measured

Telekom Sicherheitstacho

Attacks on Deutsche Telekom's honeypots on a world map in real time. A different sensor network from the DShield data in the Dreier Gauge; no level and no action part.

Operator: Deutsche Telekom · Upkeep: automatic, honeypots · checked on 26 Nov 2026

SANS Internet Storm Centerin the Dreier Gauge

InfoCon level, daily expert posts (diaries) and scan data. InfoCon and DShield are components of the Dreier Gauge. Content under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Operator: SANS Internet Storm Center · Upkeep: automatic and editorial · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Shadowserver Dashboard

Statistics on IoT devices and honeypot attacks. According to the site, funded by the UK FCDO and with EU funds. The site uses analytics cookies with consent.

Operator: Shadowserver Foundation · Upkeep: automatic, sensors and partners · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Cloudflare Radar

Internet traffic and trends as well as outages, measured in Cloudflare's network. A view of the network itself, not of vulnerabilities.

Operator: Cloudflare · Upkeep: automatic, own measurements · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Vulnerabilities

CISA: Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV)in the Dreier Gauge

The catalogue of vulnerabilities known to be exploited, also as CSV and JSON. It is the basis of several components of the Dreier Gauge.

Operator: CISA (USA) · Upkeep: official, ongoing · checked on 26 Nov 2026

EUVD: EU-Schwachstellendatenbankin the Dreier Gauge

The EU repository for vulnerabilities, with lists of critical and of exploited entries. The EU-KEV component reads the exploited ones. Rated critical does not mean the flaw is being exploited.

Operator: ENISA (EU) · Upkeep: official, ongoing · checked on 26 Nov 2026

FIRST: EPSS

A model estimating the probability that a published flaw will be exploited in the next 30 days. Updated daily, as CSV and via API. A forecast, not part of the level.

Operator: FIRST (EPSS-Arbeitsgruppe), Scores von Empirical Security · Upkeep: automatic, daily · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Ransomware and malware

ransomware.live

Tracks ransomware groups and their victims, with counters for 24 hours to 30 days, sectors and countries. On the day of the check it listed 34,564 victims and 429 groups, as of 25 Nov 2026. API, RSS and terms of use available.

Operator: Julien Mousqueton (Sicherheitsforscher) · Upkeep: automatic, leak-site monitoring · checked on 26 Nov 2026

abuse.ch

Platforms for malicious URLs (URLhaus), malware samples (MalwareBazaar) and indicators (ThreatFox). Free, aimed at professionals. The Feodo Tracker dataset has been empty since Operation Endgame.

Operator: abuse.ch, mit Spamhaus · Upkeep: automatic, community · checked on 26 Nov 2026

Notes

Hand-kept lists can have gaps or go stale. Where a site collects incidents, that does not mean every incident is covered.

Missing a good site? Suggestions welcome via the contact notice. We only add what we have checked.
